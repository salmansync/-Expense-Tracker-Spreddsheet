## 📖 Overview
A professional financial tracking system built to manage personal finances in **Bangladeshi Taka (BDT)**. This tool provides a high-level overview of net worth while maintaining granular control over daily expenditures and diverse income streams.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Yearly Dashboard:** Automated tracking of Total Net Worth and Yearly Expenses.
* **Income Source Segmentation:** Track earnings across Salary, Bkash, Rent, and more.
* **Dynamic Visualizations:** * **Donut Chart:** Real-time Net Worth breakdown.
    * **Monthly Bar Charts:** Visualizing income trends.
* **Asset Management:** Integrated balance tracking for Cash, Bank Accounts (City/Islamic/Dutch), and Digital Wallets (Bkash/Nagad/Rocket).

## 🛠️ How to Use (For Non-Tech Users)

1. **Open the Link:** Click the big green **"USE THIS TEMPLATE"** button at the top.
2. **Make a Copy:** Once the sheet opens, go to **File > Make a copy**. This saves it to your own Google Drive.
3. **Setup:** Go to the **"ACCOUNTS (ASSETS)"** table and type in your current balances for Bank, Bkash, and Cash.
4. **Track Daily:** Every time you spend money, log it in the specific month's tab (e.g., February). The dashboard will update automatically!

---

## 💻 Tech & Methodology
* **Tool:** Google Sheets / Microsoft Excel
* **Logic:** Multi-sheet data validation, SUM functions, and dynamic charting.
* **Accounting Logic:**
    $$\text{Total Net Worth} = \sum (\text{Assets}) - \sum (\text{Liabilities})$$
